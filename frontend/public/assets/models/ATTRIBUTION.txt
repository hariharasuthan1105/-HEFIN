ANATOMICAL BRAIN
Source: Brain Project by itayinbarr, https://github.com/itayinbarr/brainproject
Published source mesh: https://itayinbarr.github.io/brainproject/models/brain.glb
Upstream anatomy: Z-Anatomy contributors (https://www.z-anatomy.com/),
based on BodyParts3D, copyright The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), Japan
(https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/).

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode
Full upstream attribution/license notice: BRAIN-SOURCE-LICENSE.txt in this folder.

HEFIN modifications: selected 176 cortical/cerebellar/brainstem structures;
removed hidden internal anatomy; normalized scale and origin; reduced larger
meshes to 48% of source faces; smooth shading; material batching; cyan/violet
materials; decorative cortical paths and nodes; Draco-compressed GLB export.
The source's atlas-registered deep nuclei and tracts are not used in this export.
Decorative paths are illustrative, not measured neuronal connectivity.
Gross anatomical illustration only; not a diagnostic or surgical model.

HOSPITAL
Original HEFIN architectural approximation modeled in Blender for this project,
informed by the supplied Smart Hospital and AI Brain Meets Future Healthcare
images. No third-party hospital mesh was used. The scene represents a fictional
concept, not an operating HEFIN hospital. Geometry is not an exact reconstruction
of the supplied images. Modeling scripts and editable Blender sources are in
the repository's modeling/ directory.

BRANDING AND REFERENCE IMAGES
HEFIN logo and original references supplied by the project owner. Original
logo pixels and aspect ratio are preserved. No unrelated hospital branding is used.
